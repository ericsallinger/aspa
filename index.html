Our Mission

To apply the principles of the medical field of Psychiatry to advance the mental health and performance of those involved in human spaceflight and civil aviation.

Our Vision

To be an international resource and leader in aerospace psychiatric knowledge and best practice in all flight environments.

What is Aerospace Psychiatry?

***Aerospace psychiatry is a multidisciplinary medical field dedicated to the mental health and well-being of individuals involved in human spaceflight and aviation.

As humanity ventures further into space, the psychological challenges faced by spaceflight personnel become increasingly complex. Likewise, in aviation there is a growing need for clinicians with specialized knowledge and awareness of the complexities of this environment.

The field addresses unique mental health challenges through appropriate research, individualized treatment, and proactive mental health support.
