Core Values

Excellence

Commitment to the highest standards of clinical practice, research, policy and education in aerospace mental health.

Innovation

Embracing the best modern and informed methods to address the mental health needs of aerospace personnel.

Compassion

Promoting empathetic individualized care to support the psychological well-being of aerospace personnel.

Collaboration

Fostering interdisciplinary partnerships to integrate complementary perspectives and enhance mental health outcomes.

Resilience

Promoting psychological resilience with respect to the demands of space flight and aviation.
