Join AsPA

Membership is open to professionals and trainees from all medical specialties and allied health fields, as well as non-clinicians actively engaged or interested in the human side of aerospace medicine.

Help shape a growing field.

AsPA welcomes both established practitioners and those just beginning their training, recognizing that the future of the field will be built by every level of contributor.

  • Connect with a global network of aerospace mental health professionals
  • Access peer-reviewed research and emerging clinical guidelines
  • Attend AsPA events and contribute to the direction of the field
  • Eligibility — for Active members — to vote and serve on the Board of Directors

Membership Options

Active

$75/ year

Psychiatrists fully trained and/or certified by institutions and specialty boards recognized by AsMA and the Board of Directors. Includes full voting privileges and eligibility to serve on the Board of Directors.

Affiliate

$50/ year

Non-psychiatrist physicians, psychologists, nurse practitioners, nurses, allied health professionals, and professionals in STEM-based and other fields which support the goals and activities of aerospace psychiatry.

International

$50/ year

Fully trained and/or certified individuals who would otherwise fit into the Active or Affiliate category but who reside and practice outside the United States. Privileges follow their corresponding category.

Trainee

No Fee

Students and candidates in post-graduate clinical programs (MD/DO, Masters, Doctoral, Fellowship) whose training comprises 50% or more of their professional activity. Not assessed membership dues.
