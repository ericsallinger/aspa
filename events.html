Past Events

June 2025 · Atlanta

AsMA 2025 — Inaugural Meeting

AsPA held its inaugural meeting during AsMA 2025 at the Hyatt Regency Atlanta on Wednesday, June 4, from 2–4 PM EDT. Dr. Nick Kanas, MD presented “A Career in Space Behavioral Health Research: Past, Present and Future,” and was presented with Honorary Membership by Dr. Philip Brady alongside Founding Officers Dr. C. Dukes, Dr. P. Brady, and Dr. B. Spyropoulos.

2025

ICASM 2025

AsPA was represented at the International Congress of Aviation and Space Medicine in 2025, joining the global aerospace medicine community for sessions on flight crew mental health, fitness for duty, and the psychiatric demands of the modern aviation environment. ***

2025

OSMED Innovation Summit 2025

Dr. Dukes spoke about psychological resilience and the concept of Earth Attachment at the OSMED Innovation Summit 2025, exploring how the bond to Earth shapes the mental health and adaptation of those traveling beyond it.
